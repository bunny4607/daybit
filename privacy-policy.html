Daybit Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Daybit is a task tracker for your daily, weekly and monthly tasks. It was built to keep your tasks on your phone and nowhere else.

What we collect

Nothing. Daybit does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Your tasks

The tasks you create are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database (AES-256). The encryption key is protected by your phone's Android Keystore. Daybit does not request internet access, so your tasks cannot be sent anywhere.

Daybit opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your tasks are not copied to Google's servers or to other devices. If you uninstall the app or reset your phone, your tasks are deleted permanently.

Home screen widget

If you add the Daybit widget, it shows today's tasks on your home screen. That information stays on your device.

Children

Daybit is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
